## Feedback questions

1. **First impression.** In the first 10 seconds on the home page, what did you think the site was for? How did it look to you? (1 = confusing/unappealing, 5 = clear and appealing)

2. **Where to start.** Was it obvious where to click first? If you hesitated, what were you looking for?

3. **Level buttons.** After you clicked *Start* or *Begin* for a level, did you land where you expected? Could you find the other pages (lab, quizzes, Professor Qubit) and get back to the home page?

4. **Readability.** Was the text easy to read on the dark blue background, including small gray text, buttons, and charts? Point out anything that was hard to read.

5. **Lessons and confusing spots.** Did the step-by-step lesson walkthrough help you understand the idea? Which step or part of the site felt confusing, too long, or unfinished? Was there anything you expected to find but couldn't?

6. **Right level.** Which level did you try, and did the content feel right for that age and experience? (too easy / about right / too hard)

7. **Device.** What did you use? (phone / tablet / laptop / desktop, and which browser) Did anything look broken or cut off?

8. **Recommend.** How likely are you to recommend Qubit Path AI to a student you know? (0 = not at all, 10 = definitely) What one change would make that number higher?
